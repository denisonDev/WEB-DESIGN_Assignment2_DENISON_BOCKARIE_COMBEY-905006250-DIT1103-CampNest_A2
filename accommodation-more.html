Blue Dot

Blue House is designed for students seeking a straightforward, comfortable, and practical place to stay during their education. Student accommodation does not always need to be elaborate; what matters most is whether the property provides a suitable living environment, useful facilities, and a location that fits the student's everyday needs. The accommodation concept focuses on essential features that support student routines. A comfortable bedroom, adequate storage, and access to bathroom facilities can help residents organize their daily lives.

Location: 15C Pipe Line Street, Freetown
