ABOUT CAMP NEST

Making student accommodation easier.

Our Story

Finding a suitable place to live is an important part of every student's journey. CampNest was created to make the search for student accommodation clearer and easier. We bring accommodation information together so students can explore different options and make informed decisions about where they stay.

Our goal is to help students spend less time struggling to find a place and more time focusing on learning, personal growth, and campus life.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make student accommodation information easier to find and understand. We aim to connect students with suitable living options through a simple and welcoming platform.

WHY CAMP NEST?

Student-focused
